# Kadro Website Design Contract

## Design Read

A practical B2B service landing page for busy local operators, with a precise, masculine, low-BS business-assistant language, leaning toward Linear/Vercel restraint with a warmer service-business edge.

## Dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 6
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 3
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 4

## Visual Theme

Kadro should look like an operator's command layer, not an AI startup. The design is dark, structured, decisive, and practical. It uses a restrained electric utility accent to signal action, but avoids neon spectacle.

## Color Tokens

- --bg: #0f1110
- --surface: #171a18
- --surface-raised: #20241f
- --fg: #f4f5f2
- --muted: #a8afa5
- --border: rgba(255,255,255,0.09)
- --accent: #d6ff4f
- --accent-ink: #11140d

## Typography

Use Geist-like system sans for the site, with tight display tracking and clear body rhythm. Use monospace sparingly for workflow labels, status tags, and proof details.

## Components

- Cards: dark translucent surfaces with thin white borders, not heavy shadows.
- CTAs: one strong accent CTA per screen.
- Workflow blocks: should feel like operational handoffs, not decorative feature cards.
- Badges: small, all-caps, positive tracking.

## Anti-Patterns

- No AI purple/blue gradients.
- No fake metrics.
- No emojis as feature icons.
- No generic three-card SaaS feature block without operational specificity.
- No “AI transformation” language.
